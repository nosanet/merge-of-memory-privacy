In short: MergeOfMemory does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no in-app purchase. Your game progress is stored only on your own device and is never uploaded anywhere.

This Privacy Policy explains how Llama Studio Games (“we”, “us”, “our”) handles information when you use the Android application MergeOfMemory (the “App”). This policy applies only to the App and not to other websites, applications, or services.

By installing or using the App, you agree with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not require registration and there is no user account, login, or user-generated public content.

The App does not access the network, so no information about you or your device leaves it. Specifically, the App:

  • does not read any advertising identifier or device identifier;
  • does not request contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone, SMS, call log, or files;
  • does not create or read a user account, and does not use any social login.

The App stores the following game data in your device’s private app storage, so that the game can resume where you left it:

  • Game progress: current level, coins, discovered items, best merge and combo results, placed items on the game field, and which chapters and tiers you have seen;
  • Tasks and rewards: active tasks and their rewards, daily reward streak, and the last time a reward was claimed;
  • Upgrades and boosters: purchased upgrade levels and the time remaining on running boosters;
  • Settings: your language choice and your sound and music toggles.

This data is game progress only. It is not linked to you, your device, or your identity in any way, and it is not sent to us.

2. How We Use the Information Stored on Your Device

The locally stored data is used for one purpose only: to run the App on your device, so that your progress, tasks, upgrades, and settings are preserved between sessions. We cannot read this data, because it never leaves your device.

3. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any information, because we do not collect any. There is no data to disclose.

The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or attribution SDKs, and it embeds no third-party component that transmits data. The only bundled third-party code is an animation library that runs entirely on your device.

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or a governmental request, or to protect our rights, property, safety, or the safety of users. In the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, any information would be transferred to a successor subject to this Privacy Policy or a policy providing comparable protection.

4. No Advertising

The App contains no advertisements, no rewarded ads, and no ad networks. It does not request, read, or share any advertising identifier, and it does not perform cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. No Analytics and No Crash Reporting

The App does not measure usage. It does not send launch counts, session data, screen views, events, error reports, or crash reports to us or to any third party. We have no visibility into how, when, or how long you play.

6. No In-App Purchases

The App contains no in-app purchases and no payment functionality. It is free, contains no ads, and does not process purchases or payments of any kind, so it never sees your payment or card details.

7. Permissions

The App requests no Android permissions. It does not ask for storage, camera, microphone, contacts, location, phone state, or network access.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Game data is stored only in your device’s private app storage. It is never backed up to our servers, because we have no servers.

The data is deleted permanently and irreversibly when you uninstall the App, clear the App’s data in Android settings, or reset or replace your device. There is no cloud copy and no way for us to restore your progress for you, so please note this before uninstalling the App or changing your device.

9. International Data Transfers

None. Because no information is transmitted off your device, no data is transferred to any country or to any provider.

10. Security

Because the App neither collects nor transmits information, there is no transmission that could be intercepted, and no personal data is exposed over the network. Saved data lives in your device’s private app storage, which Android isolates from other apps. As with any software on a device, data on a compromised or rooted device, or one shared with another person, can be accessed by someone with access to that device.

11. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 years of age, or under the age of digital consent applicable in your jurisdiction.

Because the App collects no information from anyone, children do not provide us any personal information, and no verifiable parental consent is required for the App to operate. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at llamastudiogames@gmail.com, and we will take reasonable steps to investigate.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, or to complain to a supervisory authority.

Because we hold no information about you, we cannot provide a copy of your data or delete a record on our side: none exists. To remove the data the App keeps, do any of the following yourself, at any time and without any action from us:

  • uninstall the App;
  • clear the App’s data in Android settings;
  • erase all data with a factory reset of your device.

If you have any questions about this policy or about the data stored on your device, contact us at llamastudiogames@gmail.com.
